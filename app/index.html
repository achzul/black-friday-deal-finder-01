How these deals were checked

Every deal on this page was found and checked by a person on Friday 27 November 2026. Each card shows the sale price, the usual price, the discount, the store, a link to the product at that store, the exact time the price was checked (UTC), and the page where the price was read. A verdict on each deal says whether it is really a bargain: New low means the lowest price the evidence supports, Strong means a genuinely good price, Routine means a normal sale price, and Inflated means the “usual” price looks padded. Prices change fast on Black Friday — always confirm the price at the store before you buy.

FridayFinds is an independent deal guide. Prices in US dollars. Store names and product names belong to their owners; links go straight to the store.
